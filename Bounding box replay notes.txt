Play Video with bounding boxes.mp4 to see Joy's saved annotations over the

The annotated copy is 28.653625 seconds long. It has 687 video frames at
24000/1001 frames per second. The original 640 x 360 image is preserved
inside a 640 x 408 canvas. The added 48-pixel strip explains the replay.
The source watermark remains visible.

T1 through T7 identify the seven exported tracks. They are display names,
not object classes. Colors and track labels were added for this replay.
All 108 saved coordinate values remain unchanged in the source data.
Positions between saved points are calculated with linear interpolation:
each box moves evenly between the two stored positions.

TIMING
The export uses 25 frames per second, while the source video uses about
23.976. This replay maps saved playback times to the actual video times.
It does not treat annotation frame numbers as decoded video frame numbers.
Early sample frames show the boxes over the expected cars. This supports
the time-based mapping but does not establish full annotation accuracy.

TRACK ENDS AND REVIEW
T1 and T2 have disabled endpoints. Their boxes stop after those endpoints.
T3, T4, T5, T6, and T7 end with enabled=true. Their final box positions are
therefore held. Dashed boxes and an asterisk show these held positions.
This is a visible review aid; it does not change the saved coordinates.

Some boxes remain over empty road after cars leave the image. In the
3.63-second and 8.84-second samples, T5 also has an empty box before its
last saved point. Review track timing and lifespan settings in Label
Studio before calling this a finished or approved annotation project.
No tracking corrections or new labels were invented for the replay.

IMPLEMENTATION REFERENCES

Its lifespan state uses the nearest preceding saved point. VideoRectangle
interpolates shape properties and holds the last shape when no later point
exists. This copy uses continuous playback times for the reconstruction.
